The Lull voice cast

speaking characters, each with one voice used for every line they say. Each voice is matched to the character's age, build, temperament and home land: people from the same place share an accent, so Larkfold sounds like Larkfold and the Guild sounds like Vantry.

The boys Finch and Tam are voiced by young women, as animated films do: the voice model cannot make a convincing child. Elders speak slower and with an uneven waver.
